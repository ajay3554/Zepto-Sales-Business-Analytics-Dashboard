# Zepto Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard for Zepto sales and business analytics, featuring sales trends, category analysis, top products, city-wise sales, order status, and interactive filters.

## Dashboard Preview

![Zepto Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Dashboard Views

- Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales Share by Category
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Sales by City
- Order Status
- Interactive Year, Month, Category, Sub-Category and City filters

## Tools

- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
